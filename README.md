# PingMapPlayPong

PingMapPlayPong is an app designed to make the use of free outdoor ping-pong tables more convenient and enjoyable. Whether you're looking for a nearby table, tracking your game scores, or challenging your friends, PingMapPlayPong has got you covered!

## About The Project

### PingMapPlayPong

PingMapPlayPong aims to enhance the experience of using free outdoor ping-pong tables by providing users with tools to easily find tables, save favorites, and log matches. Users can create profiles, register their use of a table, record match results, and participate in group games. The app also plans to introduce features like table reviews and ratings, making it a comprehensive tool for all ping-pong enthusiasts.

## Key Features

- **Table Finder:** Search for free outdoor ping-pong tables on a map.
- **Favorites:** Save your favorite tables for quick access.
- **User Profiles:** Create a profile to track your activities and preferences.
- **Match Logging:** Register your use of a table and log match results.
- **Group Matches:** Organize and track group matches with multiple players.
- **Leaderboard:** Compete with others and see who tops the charts at your favorite tables.
- **Table Reviews and Ratings (Coming Soon):** Add comments and rate tables to help others find the best spots.

## Usage

PingMapPlayPong is designed for anyone who enjoys playing ping-pong outdoors and wants to make the most of the free tables available. The app is perfect for players who want to keep track of their games, find the best spots, and connect with other players in their community.

1. **Search for Tables:** Use the map feature to find nearby tables.
2. **Save Favorites:** Bookmark your favorite locations for quick access.
3. **Log Matches:** Register your games and keep a history of your scores.
4. **Challenge Friends:** Set up group matches and see who comes out on top.
5. **Stay Updated:** Soon, you’ll be able to leave feedback on tables and check out reviews from other players.
